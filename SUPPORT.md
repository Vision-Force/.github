# Support

Vision Force Studio is a one person fan project, run as a hobby. One person
answers, so please pick the channel that matches what you need.

## Email, for anything needing a human answer

**contact@visionforceofficial.com**

This is the monitored channel. Use it for account problems, press and
collaboration, takedown or legal questions, anything private, and anything that
needs a written answer you can point back to.

Replies take days rather than hours. If you have had no reply after two weeks,
send a plain follow up in the same thread.

Security issues do not go here as a normal mail. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
and mark the subject line.

## Discord, the social space

<https://discord.gg/ABmyD3NRGV>

Discord is where the community talks: builds, clips, track ideas, and general
help from other players. It is a social space, not a support desk. Nobody is on
rota there, and a message can scroll past unread.

If your question needs an official answer, send the email as well. Do not send
the same question to both and then a third time in a thread.

## Status page

<https://status.visionforceofficial.com>

Check here first if a site or service is not responding. If the page is green and
you still cannot connect, that is worth an email.

## Bugs and feature ideas

- Bugs in a Vision Force repository: open a bug report through the issue form.
- Gameplay and roadmap ideas: post and vote on the public roadmap at
  <https://visionforceofficial.com>. Sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub.
- Changes to code, tooling or documentation: see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Follow progress

## What support cannot cover

Vision Force Studio cannot help with Epic Games accounts, Fortnite itself, or
Unreal Engine licensing. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.
Fortnite and Unreal are trademarks of Epic Games, Inc.

Rocket Racing Reborn is an original re-implementation of network services and is early, in
development. The studio does not distribute game clients or builds, and cannot
help you find one. Requests for downloads will not be answered.
