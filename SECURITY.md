# Security Policy

Vision Force Studio takes security reports seriously. This page is the real
process, not a formality.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **contact@visionforceofficial.com**. Put "Security" in the subject line.

Do not open a public issue, do not post in Discord, and do not demonstrate the
problem against anyone else's account or data.

If you use PGP or would prefer an encrypted channel, say so in a first plain
message and one will be arranged.

## What to include

The more of this you can give, the faster it can be confirmed.

- The affected site, service or repository, and the exact URL or endpoint.
- The type of issue, for example authentication bypass, injection, exposed
  secret, privilege escalation, or insecure direct object reference.
- Steps to reproduce, in order, with the requests or inputs used.
- Any proof of concept code, script or screenshot.
- What an attacker could obtain or do with it, in your own assessment.
- Whether the issue is already public anywhere.

Please report only what you found using your own accounts and your own data.

## What to expect

Vision Force Studio is run by one person as a hobby project, so the timings
below are commitments about effort, not a staffed rota.

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement that the mail arrived | within 5 days |
| First assessment, confirmed or not reproduced | within 14 days |
| Fix or a written plan with a date | within 90 days of confirmation |

If you have not heard anything after 14 days, send a plain follow up. Mail does
get lost.

## Coordinated disclosure

The request is simple: give the fix a chance to ship before the report goes
public.

- Keep the details private until a fix is released, or until 90 days have passed
  since the first report, whichever comes first.
- If the issue is being actively exploited, that clock does not apply. Say so and
  it will be treated as urgent.
- You will be credited by the name or handle you choose when the fix is
  announced, unless you ask not to be.
- There is no bug bounty. No money is offered and none should be expected.

## In scope

- The Vision Force websites, including visionforceofficial.com and
  status.visionforceofficial.com.
- Account sign in on those sites, including the Google, Discord and GitHub sign
  in paths, and the roadmap voting and suggestion features.
- Any service or API operated by Vision Force Studio at a
  visionforceofficial.com hostname.
- Source code published in repositories under
  <https://github.com/Vision-Force>, including Rocket Racing Reborn.
- The Vision Force Discord server's own configuration and bots, where operated by
  the studio.

## Out of scope

- Anything belonging to Epic Games, including Fortnite, Unreal Engine, UEFN,
  Epic accounts, and Epic's own services. Vision Force Studio is not affiliated
  with or endorsed by Epic Games and cannot receive or act on reports about
  Epic's systems. Report those to Epic Games directly.
- Third party platforms and their infrastructure, including GitHub, Discord,
  Google, YouTube, TikTok, and any hosting or CDN provider. Report those through
  the platform's own disclosure programme.
- Archived game clients and any build a player supplies themselves. Vision Force
  Studio does not distribute them and cannot patch them.
- Findings from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact, missing security
  headers on their own, and reports whose only content is a tool score.
- Social engineering of studio members or community volunteers, physical
  attacks, denial of service and volumetric testing, and spam or rate limit
  probing against live services.

## Please do not

- Access, modify or delete data that is not yours.
- Degrade a live service for other players.
- Publish an unfixed issue for attention.

Good faith research within this policy will not be met with a complaint or a
takedown.
